# Reading Guide

## 1. Project Outline

**File:** [SpecialCourse_DeconstructedDWM_SJAN.pdf](SpecialCourse_DeconstructedDWM_SJAN.pdf)

**What it is:** A draft outline of how to test the passive-tracer assumption related to the mean wake deficit and meandering.

**Information to use:**

- Use the outline as inspiration for the project's approach to testing that assumption.
- Treat it as a draft: some aspects may need further formalization.

## 2. Steiner et al. (2026)

**File:** [Steiner, J. et al., 2026 - A multi-fidelity model benchmark for wake steering of a large turbine.pdf](Steiner,%20J.%20et%20al.,%202026%20-%20A%20multi-fidelity%20model%20benchmark%20for%20wake%20steering%20of%20a%20large%20turbine.pdf)

**What it is:** A comparison of models of different fidelity, including LES and DWM, for wake steering of the IEA 22 MW reference turbine in a conventionally neutral atmospheric boundary layer. According to the guidance accompanying the supplied LES data, the paper uses the same simulation from which the project's LES data are provided.

**Information to use:**

- Start here as the recommended first literature reference for the project.
- Use the simulation setup as the reference for understanding the supplied LES data. In the published article, Sections 2.1 and 2.2 describe the turbine, inflow, numerical setup, and LES method.
- Use Section 2.4 of the published article for a detailed explanation of the DWM method, including the passive-tracer assumption, filtering of the background flow for meandering, wake deficit evolution, and wake-added turbulence.
- Use the LES-DWM comparison to put the project's analysis of wake dynamics and meandering in context.

## 3. Trujillo et al. (2011)

**File:** [Trujillo et al., 2011 - Light detection and ranging measurements of wake dynamics  Part II  two‐dimensional.pdf](Trujillo%20et%20al.,%202011%20-%20Light%20detection%20and%20ranging%20measurements%20of%20wake%20dynamics%20%20Part%20II%20%20two%E2%80%90dimensional.pdf)

**What it is:** A measurement-based study of the correlation between inflow and wake position. It also directly compares wake deficit and TI in fixed and moving frames of reference.

**Information to use:**

- The relationship between inflow and wake position, as a measurement-based reference for the project. Similar studies have been done using LES.
- Figure 8: the comparison of wake deficit in fixed and moving frames.
- Figure 9: the comparison of TI in fixed and moving frames.

## 4. Andersen et al. (2013)

**File:** [Andersen et al., 2013 - Simulation of the inherent turbulence and wake interaction inside an infinitely.pdf](Andersen%20et%20al.,%202013%20-%20Simulation%20of%20the%20inherent%20turbulence%20and%20wake%20interaction%20inside%20an%20infinitely.pdf)

**What it is:** A paper that is not primarily about DWM, but reports how the wake center was found.

**Information to use:**

- Focus on Section 3.3.
- Use its description of how the wake center was found, while consulting the later paper below for a clearer explanation.
- Do not rely on Figure 10: it is incorrect.

## 5. Andersen et al. (2018)

**File:** [Andersen et al., 2018 - Instantaneous response and mutual interaction between wind turbine and flow.pdf](Andersen%20et%20al.,%202018%20-%20Instantaneous%20response%20and%20mutual%20interaction%20between%20wind%20turbine%20and%20flow.pdf)

**What it is:** A later paper with a clearer explanation of how the wake center is found, with correlations between the wake center and, for example, power.

**Information to use:**

- The clearer explanation of wake-center identification.
- The correlation between wake center and power as an example of relating wake position to turbine response.


## 6. Reinwardt et al. (2021)

**File:** [Reinwardt et al., 2021 - Validation of the dynamic wake meandering model with respect to loads and power production.pdf](Reinwardt%20et%20al.,%202021%20-%20Validation%20of%20the%20dynamic%20wake%20meandering%20model%20with%20respect%20to%20loads%20and%20power%20production.pdf)

**What it is:** A paper on recalibrating DWM based on measurements, including different submodels.

**Information to use:**

- The measurement-based recalibration of DWM.
- The different submodels included in that recalibration.
